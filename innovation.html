Areas of interest

The questions that keep coming back.

These are the threads that keep getting returned to. They are not product roadmaps. They are the questions that keep producing useful answers.

01

Emerging AI

New models arrive faster than most teams can evaluate them. Testing happens early, on real tasks, and each generation gets compared honestly to the last. That habit keeps recommendations grounded in evidence rather than in press releases.

02

Intelligent automation

Automation becomes interesting when it handles judgement, not just repetition. The prototypes here read context, choose between routes and report back clearly when something needs a human decision.

03

Modular software ecosystems

Systems get designed in parts small enough to be understood and replaced independently. The result is software that can outlive the specific tools it was first written with.

04

Experimental digital products

Some experiments become products. CSIC, the desktop assistant, is one example. These projects are built in the open and documented honestly, including the parts that did not work as expected.

05

New approaches to engineering

How AI changes the act of building software is genuinely interesting. Tooling, review practice and the shape of a development team all shift when the assistant becomes part of the loop.

How it is run

A few rules that keep it useful.

Experimentation without discipline becomes noise. These four habits keep the work from drifting into it.

Build before you argue

A working prototype ends a debate faster than a long document. Build the smallest version that can actually be judged.

Keep the experiment honest

What failed gets recorded as carefully as what worked. Half the value of an experiment is the list of dead ends it saves someone else.

Ship what is useful

An experiment that solves a real problem graduates into product work. One that only looks impressive does not.

Stay replaceable

Nothing becomes a dependency it cannot be swapped out of later. Models, services and tools all change.

The point of it

An idea only matters when it reaches the people doing the work. The experimenting exists to shorten that distance.
